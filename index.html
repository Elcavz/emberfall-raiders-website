How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down

Move

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your hero. Keyboard: WASD or arrows.

Attack

Fully automatic. Your hero hits the nearest enemy in range, so you can focus on positioning.

Aim your skills

Drag a skill button to aim, just like in a MOBA: the aim shows on the ground. Let go to cast, drag onto CANCEL to call it off, or tap to auto-aim. Keyboard: Q E F.

Dash & Potion

Dash out of danger, or drink a potion to heal. Keyboard: Space / R.

Tip: A boss waits at the end of every 5th stage. Save your ultimate for it.
Tip: Perk ranks stack. Doubling down on Multistrike or Spirit Blades makes your hero very strong.
Tip: Chain kills to build your combo for up to +50% gold, and chase the Treasure Sprite before it escapes.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Emberfall Raiders free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play, with no ads. Optional gem packs are sold through Google Play. Every hero can also be unlocked with gold earned by playing.

Do I need an internet connection?

Solo runs work offline. Co-op needs an internet connection to reach the party server. When you are online, the game also checks for updates: if your version is too old, it asks you to update before you can play.

Which phones does it run on?

Any phone or tablet with Android 7.0 or newer. The download is about 5 MB.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your phone. It is included in Android's backup and phone-to-phone transfer. If you uninstall the app or clear its data without a backup, your progress is lost.

Can I play with friends who use the KiX app?

Yes. The Android app and the KiX version share the same co-op server. Share your 5-digit room code and your friend can join from either one.

I bought gems but didn't receive them. What now?

Gems are added as soon as Google Play confirms the payment. If the app closed during a purchase, just open it again and the purchase is delivered. "Pending" payments arrive once they complete. The Starter Bundle is restored automatically after a reinstall. Still missing something? Contact us using the address below.

Can I install the APK instead of using Google Play?

Yes. Download the APK above and allow "Install unknown apps" when your phone asks. In-app purchases only work in the version installed from Google Play.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Applies to the Emberfall Raiders Android app (com.elcavz.emberfallraiders) and this website

The short version

  • No accounts of our own. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number or any other personal details. You can give your hero a nickname if you like.
  • Optional Google Play Games. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Google stores your achievements, leaderboard scores and a cloud copy of your progress in your Play Games profile.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics tools and does not use your advertising ID.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone, and also in your Google Play Games account if you are signed in, so it follows you to a new phone. We run no game-save server of our own.
  • Co-op shares only what a match needs (your hero name, hero and best stage), and only while you are in a party.
  • Payments go through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

1. Who we are

Emberfall Raiders is developed by Elcavz ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Emberfall Raiders Android app and this website handle, why, and who it is shared with. If you play Emberfall Raiders inside the KiX / GameTok app, KiX's own privacy policy also applies to the information KiX processes, such as your KiX profile and cloud save.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device and, if you are signed in, in Google Play Games.
  • To unlock your achievements and post your scores to the Google Play Games leaderboards.
  • To connect you with other players in co-op and keep your party in sync.
  • To deliver the items you buy and to restore one-time purchases after a reinstall.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, build profiles about you, or use your information for any other purpose.

4. Who information is shared with

  • Other players in your co-op party see your hero name, hero, best stage and your actions in the raid. Please don't use your real name or other personal details as your hero name.
  • Hosting provider: our co-op server and this website are hosted by Render (Render privacy policy). It processes connection data so the service can run.
  • Google: Google Play processes purchases, and Android System WebView (made by Google) displays the game. Android backup is also run by Google. If you use Google Play Games, Google Play Games Services stores your saved game, achievements and leaderboard scores, and also collects diagnostics to keep its service stable. See the Google Privacy Policy and what Play Games Services collects.
  • Other players can see your Play Games gamer name, leaderboard scores and achievements, depending on your Play Games profile visibility settings.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, although we hold almost nothing that could be disclosed.

5. Deleting your Emberfall Raiders data

Emberfall Raiders (by Elcavz) has no accounts of its own, so there is no account to close. Your data is in up to two places, and you can delete each one yourself at any time:

  1. Progress on your phone: uninstall Emberfall Raiders, or open Settings → Apps → Emberfall Raiders → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your gold, gems, heroes, upgrades, records, hero name and settings immediately.
  2. Google Play Games data (only if you signed in to Play Games): open the Google Play Games app or your Play Games profile in the Play Store, go to its Settings, choose Delete Play Games account & data, and delete the data for Emberfall Raiders only (or your whole Play Games profile). This deletes your cloud save, achievements and leaderboard scores for the game. Google carries out the deletion under the Google Privacy Policy. You can also do this from your Google Account.

Delete both if you want to start over: if you only clear the phone, the cloud copy brings your progress back the next time you sign in.

6. Security

Connections between the app and our co-op server are encrypted (secure WebSocket over TLS). Google Play Games Services encrypts its data in transit (HTTPS). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

7. Children

Emberfall Raiders is a general-audience game. The app does not ask for any personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because we do not keep personal information linked to you, there is usually nothing to hand over. You can always contact us to ask.

9. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to send them. The APK download is served directly from this website.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
